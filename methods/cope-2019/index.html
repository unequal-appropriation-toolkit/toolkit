The Wealth of (Some) Nations: Imperialism and the Mechanics of Value Transfer

Zak Cope · 2019

Documentation status

Source-based preliminary profile informed by accessible book text; chapter-level numerical procedures and sources not independently audited; replication not attempted.

Research profile

Examines mechanisms of imperial value transfer, including unequal exchange of embodied labour, and their implications for international class relations.

Research object
Connects international value transfer, wage differentiation and imperial relations to the reproduction of class and consumption hierarchies.
Research design
Combines historical and theoretical political economy with quantitative discussion of labour embodied in international exchange.
Analytical techniques
Comparison of labour embodied in imports and exports alongside analysis of price, wage and monopoly relations; individual numerical estimates require source-by-source verification.
Outputs and limits of inference
Historical and theoretical claims and quantitative illustrations concerning international value transfer; not independently replicated.
