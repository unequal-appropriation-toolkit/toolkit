The Law of World Value

Alan Freeman · Manuscript

Documentation status

Author-supplied manuscript; bibliographic publication status unverified; calculations and underlying data not independently reproduced.

Research profile

Investigates persistent international monetary inequality and proposes a pluralist research programme to explain divergence; presents the Monetary Inequality Index (MII).

Research object
Examines international divergence and competing explanations of monetary inequality, including world-value and terms-of-trade arguments. The paper proposes testing explanations rather than treating the index as a causal test.
Research design
Compares population-weighted GDP per capita of defined country blocs in US dollars at current market exchange rates; distinguishes monetary measures from PPP consumption measures and productive capacity.
Analytical techniques
Monetary Inequality Index (MII): ratio of GDP per capita of a benchmark group to GDP per capita of a comparison group. Draws on Penn national accounts data extended with World Economic Outlook data; author calculations.
Outputs and limits of inference
MII time series and comparative charts of divergence, including comparisons across country groupings; not a direct estimate of value transferred.

Source basis

  • Author-supplied manuscript, The Law of World Value (version held for review; publication status not verified).
