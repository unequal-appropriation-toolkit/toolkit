Unequal Exchange: A Study of the Imperialism of Trade

Arghiri Emmanuel · 1972

Documentation status

Source-based conceptual profile using bibliographic record and Emmanuel's openly accessible 1975 elaboration; 1972 book not fully checked; no independent reproduction.

Research profile

Develops a theory of unequal exchange through international wage differences and price formation, with implications for international distribution.

Research object
International wage differentials, in the context of mobile capital and tendencies toward profit-rate equalisation, affect international prices of production and distribution.
Research design
Develops theoretical arguments and comparative price systems; the 1975 elaboration sets out algebraic and numerical examples and a modified Sraffa framework.
Analytical techniques
Theoretical price-of-production equations and comparative examples. This is not an observed international value-transfer time series.
Outputs and limits of inference
A theoretical account of international unequal exchange and its distributive implications, rather than an independently replicated monetary estimate.

Source basis

  • 1972 book bibliographic record
  • Emmanuel, Unequal Exchange Revisited (1975)
