The Structure of Global Money and World Tables of Unequal Exchange

Gernot Köhler · 1998

Documentation status

Source-based preliminary profile; article metadata and abstract verified; precise tables and calculation steps require full-text checking; replication not attempted.

Research profile

Investigates the non-homogeneous international value of money and uses international price/exchange-rate comparisons to estimate unequal exchange.

Research object
Proposes that systematic currency undervaluation and international exchange-rate structures contribute to transfers between low- and high-income countries.
Research design
Relates purchasing-power comparisons to market exchange-rate valuation and applies an exchange-rate distortion approach to international exchange.
Analytical techniques
International price-level or PPP versus market-exchange-rate comparison, with trade data used in unequal-exchange estimation; exact implementation to be documented from full text.
Outputs and limits of inference
Published world tables and estimated unequal-exchange quantities, whose numerical results have not been independently checked here.
