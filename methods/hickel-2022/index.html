Imperialist appropriation in the world economy: Drain from the global South through unequal exchange, 1990–2015

Hickel et al.

Material and ecological relations · primaryValue, prices and monetary relations

Documentation status: Methodological profile under development; replication not attempted

Methodological architecture

01 / Theoretical mechanism

Interprets net North–South appropriation of embodied resources and labour through unequal exchange.

02 / Research or estimation strategy

Reaggregates embodied-flow estimates into North–South net transfers and adds monetary valuation calculations.

03 / Analytical technique

Uses the Eora v.199.82 MRIO dataset reported by Dorninger et al. (2021), covering 1990–2015; adds monetary calculations.

04 / Measured quantities or other outputs

Net embodied transfers of materials, energy, land and labour, alongside monetary estimates.
