Unequal appropriation concerns the relations through which labour, resources, value and wealth are produced, transferred and claimed, and the mechanisms that make those relations unequal.

The Unequal Appropriation Toolkit is an open research resource for investigating unequal exchange, economic dependency and the organisation of the world economy.

The toolkit brings competing explanations, methods and evidence into conversation. It shows how different approaches define appropriation, identify inequality and substantiate their claims.

01 / The conceptual question

From unequal flows
to unequal relations.

Flows reveal patterns. Explaining appropriation requires examining the social relations, institutions and mechanisms that produce and reproduce them.

01

What is appropriated?

Research traces labour, resources, value, surplus and financial claims through distinct concepts and forms of evidence.

02

What makes it unequal?

Asymmetry must be interpreted. Which relations, mechanisms and standards make a difference evidence of unequal appropriation?

03

What can the evidence establish?

Methods illuminate some processes while leaving others unresolved. The Toolkit distinguishes what is measured, what is inferred and what remains contested.

The concept of unequal appropriation provides a common field of inquiry: how different theories identify appropriated quantities, explain unequal relations and establish their findings.
